II · The diagnosis in one paragraph

STARSPELL's core is finished and good: the cast, the beasts, the sky, the seven skies, the duels. What it lacks is breadth around the core — a calendar (things that happen on a date), a shelf (things you have earned and can look at), a second road (what to do after the dawn), and voices (the beasts and bosses as characters). GvT has all four because it shipped one small thing a day for twelve weeks. The ideas below are grouped that way, and every one of them reuses plumbing the game already has — the sky wheel, the drip conditions, the flag ledger, the rival engine, the sheet pattern — so most are S or M cards, not projects.

The 1.0 rule I used: a thing goes on the November road only if it is (a) already designed with a page, or (b) an S/M card that reuses existing plumbing, and (c) makes the first week of a stranger's play fuller. Everything that needs a new shell build (store, push) or a new art round of real size goes to 1.0.1 and later, no matter how good it is.
